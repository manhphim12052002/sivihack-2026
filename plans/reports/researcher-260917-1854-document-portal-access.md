## Bottom-line numbers (of 3,176 document-bearing lots)

Top-12 hosts here = 2,033 lots (64.0% of all document-bearing lots):
- **DIRECT** (plain HTTP, no login, no JS): subreport-elvis.de 294 + meinauftrag.rib.de 217 + evergabe-online.de 140 = **651 lots (20.5%)**
- **GATED** (login/registration/order-form required): evergabe.de 321 + dtvp.de 218 + niedersachsen 130 + brandenburg 110 + deutsche-evergabe.de 94 + vergabe-westfalen.de 93 + vergabe24.de 84 = **1,050 lots (33.1%)**

**Best-ROI approach**: plain HTTP/curl (no browser) covers **22.8%** of all document-bearing lots outright. A headless browser would only help the LANDING_ONLY bucket (**+10.5%**, reaching ~33.3%) — and even that's unconfirmed, since bieterportal.noncd.db.de and subreport.de might still gate behind login once rendered (not tested; rendering was out of scope this session). The **GATED third (33.1%)** is not a tooling problem — every one of those hosts required a company-data or account-registration form before showing files, which is explicitly out of scope regardless of browser automation.
